Quote questions

Before you send

What if I’m still deciding on a pouch format?

Select “Help me choose”. Share the product, fill weight and how the pack will be stored or used so the team can discuss suitable options with you.

Can I include artwork or a reference pack?

Yes. Mention any artwork, dielines or reference photos in your request, and our team will follow up to collect them.

Can I request pricing for several designs?

List each design and its estimated quantity in the project notes. Include different sizes or features for each version where applicable.

Material guide

Pouch materials
and finishes

Compare white, clear and metallic pouch examples in matte BOPP and gloss PET. Ask MewePak to confirm the full film specification and availability for your format.

Key takeaways

Pick a base
White gives artwork a solid ground, clear shows what’s inside, and metallic gives the pack a silver look.
Choose the sheen
Matte softens reflections. Gloss gives the pouch a brighter, shinier surface.
Check the full film structure
Ask for the proposed film’s barrier and seal data based on your product and filling process.
Test the filled pack
Evaluate your filled pouch under the conditions it will face in storage and transit.
