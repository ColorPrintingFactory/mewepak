        A little clarity

        Frequently asked
        questions.

        A few useful starting points for your packaging conversation.

        Can I start without finished artwork?+

        Yes—share what you have, such as a logo, an early design or a reference pack. Include your intended dimensions and ask which artwork files are needed for the next stage.

        What helps define the packaging specification?+

        Start with your product, fill quantity, preferred format and known filling or storage conditions. These details help frame the discussion about dimensions, materials, openings and other pack requirements.

        Does every pack follow the same production route?+

        This page shows an illustrative six-stage overview. The production route is considered for the agreed material, print and packaging format, so share your project details before the specification is confirmed.

        What should I include in my first enquiry?+

        Share your product, preferred format, approximate quantity and any artwork or dimensions you already have. Add questions about your current packaging so the conversation can focus on the details that matter.
