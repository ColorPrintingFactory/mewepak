LABELLED OR FULLY PRINTED?

Same pouch.
A different brand presence.

See how an applied label compares with a design printed directly onto the bag. Two approaches, with different ways to tell your brand story.

STOCK BAG + APPLIED LABEL

Your design, within a label.

A ready-made pouch carries a separate printed label. Your branding and product information sit within the label’s shape and size, while the rest of the bag keeps its stock appearance.

  • A focused area for your logo and key product details.
  • Different labels can identify different products or variants.
  • Label placement and application are a separate step.

FULLY & UNIQUELY PRINTED BAG

More room for your brand story.

Your artwork is printed directly onto the packaging material. Graphics, colour and product information can extend across the available print panels for a more integrated presentation.

  • Branding extends beyond the boundaries of a label.
  • Artwork can connect across front, back and printable side panels.
  • Print layout is planned around seals, folds and production requirements.

Illustrative designs show the same sample product and pouch format. Printed artwork alone does not determine the material’s barrier performance or product suitability.

The practical difference
What to compareBag with an applied labelFully custom printed bag
Design spaceDefined by the label size and where it is applied.Available printable panels, within the agreed artwork template.
Overall appearanceThe stock bag remains visible around the label.Colour, graphics and type form a coordinated packaging design.
Product variationsDifferent labels can be paired with the same stock pouch.Each variant can have its own planned printed artwork.
What to planPouch size, label material, adhesion and application.Print layout, material, artwork approval and production quantities.
Cost & timingConfirm pouch and label quantities, application and total lead time.Confirm print method, order quantity and production lead time.

Not sure which route fits? Tell us your product, artwork plans and estimated quantity. Our team can discuss relevant examples and quote the available options.

A FEW HELPFUL ANSWERS
Before you request.
Have something specific in mind?
Talk to our packaging team
What will my sample kit include?
The selection depends on your product needs and the samples currently available. Tell us which formats, finishes and features you want to compare, and our team will confirm the contents before dispatch.
Is the sample kit free?
Any kit and shipping charges will be confirmed after we review your enquiry. This page does not take payment or create an order.
Can I request samples with my own branding?
This enquiry is for available packaging examples. If you need a custom printed proof or prototype, mention it in your request so our team can discuss feasibility, costs and timing separately.
Where do you ship and how long does it take?
Share your country or region in the request. Our team will confirm shipping availability, charges and an estimated delivery schedule for your destination.
Can I use a sample to confirm product suitability?
Samples are useful for an initial review of format and finish. Final dimensions, material structure, barrier needs and compatibility with your product and filling process require separate confirmation and appropriate testing.
What happens after I review the samples?
Share your feedback with our team. We can then discuss your preferred format, material requirements, artwork, order quantity and a quotation for the next stage.
