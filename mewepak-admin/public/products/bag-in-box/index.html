        FAQ

        Still have questions? Here are the answers.

        Quick answers to common questions about structure, transport, samples, and sizing.

        How do these packaging solutions protect my product?

        We match barrier, seal, and structure choices to your product’s shelf-life and logistics requirements.

        Are these pouches suitable for transport?

        Yes. We engineer structures for puncture resistance, seal strength, and stable handling during shipping.

        Can I order a sample pack?

        Yes. We can provide stock or custom samples so you can evaluate format, finish, and fit before scaling up.

        What sizes are available?

        We offer a wide range of custom dimensions to suit fill weight, shelf impact, and production line needs.
