Designed around your product

A premium presentation.
A practical everyday pack.

A visible product window, thoughtful artwork space and a resealable structure come together in one retail-ready format.

The Premium Almonds Window Stand-Up Pouch is developed specifically for nut packaging where a natural, trustworthy, and premium look is required. The cream and deep maroon palette gives the pouch a warm grocery-shelf feel, while almond imagery and the transparent window communicate the product instantly.

This pouch is ideal for almonds, roasted nut portions, raw nuts, trail mixes, and dry fruit blends. The front panel can highlight product name, weight, benefit icons, and visible almond texture. The reverse side can be arranged for ingredients, allergen information, nutrition facts, storage directions, barcode, and supplier details.

Because nut products are often consumed across several servings, the resealable zipper makes the pack practical for home storage. The stand-up gusset also gives the pouch strong shelf posture for supermarkets, health shops, gifting ranges, and online product images.

Material options to review

Nut packaging benefits from moisture protection, aroma retention and good seal strength. The laminate can be adjusted for raw, roasted, salted, flavoured or oil-rich products.

  • PET / PEClean printing and standard dry snack protection
  • BOPP / PEA lightweight natural snack pouch finish
  • PET / VMPET / PEImproved barrier against light and moisture
  • PET / AL / PEHigher barrier nut packaging where extended freshness support is needed
  • Clear window laminateCombined with a printed cream-maroon outer design

Window compatibility and the full structure must be reviewed together. A foil or metallized laminate is not automatically a transparent-window construction.

Before you enquire

A few useful answers.

Can I change the pouch size and artwork?

Yes. The supplied design shows a 100g / 3.5 oz format. Custom sizes for 50g, 100g, 250g or larger ranges can be discussed, along with colours, photography, claims and product information. Actual dimensions and fill capacity need to be confirmed.

Which material should I choose for nuts?

The right laminate depends on the product’s oil content, oxidation sensitivity, storage conditions, expected shelf life and whether you need a visible window. Share whether the nuts are raw, roasted, salted or flavoured so the full construction can be reviewed.

Can the window have a different shape?

Custom almond window shapes and sizes are included in the options to discuss. Review the desired window together with the material and printing requirements before confirming the specification.

What information should I include in a quote request?

Include your product, fill weight or intended dimensions, estimated quantity, window and closure preferences, finish, delivery region and any target timing. You can also share artwork or ask what needs to be prepared.
