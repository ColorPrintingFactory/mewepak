        01 / Who we are

        Meet MewePak.

        A flexible packaging partner for brands launching a product, updating a range or choosing a new pack format.

        Every project starts with what goes inside the pack and how people will use it. We help you work through the format, materials, print and finishing details, then turn those choices into a brief for production.

        Our packaging concepts span food and beverages, pet care, beauty, wellness and household products.
